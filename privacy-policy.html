🔐 Ape Langa Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ape Langa may collect, use and protect information provided by customers and service providers when using the platform.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the services used, Ape Langa may collect information such as name, email address, contact details, booking information and information required to provide requested services.

2. Account Information

When a customer creates an account, authentication information may be processed through our authentication service provider.

3. Booking Information

Information related to vehicle hire, accommodation and other bookings may be stored to manage requests, confirmations, payments and customer service.

4. Payment Information

Online payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as PayHere. Ape Langa does not intentionally store customers' complete card numbers or payment passwords.

5. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Manage customer accounts
  • Process bookings and service requests
  • Communicate with customers
  • Process and verify payments
  • Improve the Ape Langa platform
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain platform security

6. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with relevant service providers when necessary to fulfil a customer's booking or requested service.

Information may also be processed by trusted third-party service providers used to operate the platform.

7. Data Security

Ape Langa will take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect customer information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

The Ape Langa website may use browser technologies such as cookies or local storage to support website functionality, preferences and temporary application data.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes and comply with applicable legal requirements.

10. Third-Party Services

Ape Langa may use third-party services including authentication, database, hosting and payment providers. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

11. Customer Rights

Customers may contact Ape Langa regarding their personal information, account information or privacy-related questions.

12. Changes to This Policy

Ape Langa may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Updated information will be published on this page.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact Ape Langa through the contact details provided on the website.
