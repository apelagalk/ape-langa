↩️ Ape Langa Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: 2026

This policy explains the general cancellation and refund process for services booked through Ape Langa.

1. Booking Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of a booking subject to the cancellation conditions applicable to that booking and the relevant service provider.

2. Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility may depend on the booking status, cancellation time, service provider policy and the circumstances of the cancellation.

3. Provider Cancellation

If a confirmed service cannot be provided by the relevant service provider, Ape Langa may assist the customer with the applicable cancellation or refund process.

4. Payment Processing

Payments made through supported online payment services may require processing time before a refund is completed.

5. Refund Method

Where a refund is approved, the refund will normally be processed through the applicable payment method or payment provider, subject to their processing procedures.

6. Non-Refundable Services

Certain bookings or services may have specific non-refundable conditions. Customers should review the applicable booking terms before making payment.

7. Service Issues

If a customer experiences a significant issue with a booked service, the customer should contact Ape Langa and provide the relevant booking information and details of the issue.

8. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds may take additional time to appear in the customer's account depending on the payment provider or financial institution.

9. Contact

For cancellation or refund questions, customers should contact Ape Langa using the contact details provided on the website.
